Privacy Policy

Bullet Express: Train Defense
Last Updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and shared when you use the Bullet Express: Train Defense mobile application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Our Privacy Commitment

Bullet Express is a single-player train defense game. Your gameplay progress, gold, train, items, and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not require an account, and we do not collect names, email addresses, or other directly identifying personal information.

Information Stored on Your Device

  • Gameplay Progress: Current level and wave, gold, your train and wagon layout, weapons and upgrades, power-up parts, season pass and daily reward progress, and tutorial state are stored locally using on-device storage (Unity PlayerPrefs).
  • Settings: Sound, music, vibration, and game speed preferences are stored locally.

Device Backups

The App does not sync progress between devices and has no account. So that reinstalling the App on the same device does not lose your progress, a small copy of it (level, gold, train, items and purchased items) is kept in that device's secure storage (on iOS, the Keychain, "this device only"). It never leaves the device and we cannot read it. On Android, the App's local data may be included in Android's standard app backup tied to your Google account; on iOS it may also be included in your own device backup (iCloud or computer backup) and come back if you set up a new device from that backup. Those copies live in your own Apple or Google account, not on our servers, and we cannot read them. You can manage them in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Backup; iOS: Settings > [your name] > iCloud).

Leaderboards & Weekly League

The App may show leaderboards (for example, a monthly ranking and a weekly league). To place you on them, the App sends a randomly generated player identifier, the anonymous device identifier described below, and your scores (such as waves cleared and highest level) to our backend hosted by Supabase. Other players see only your score and an automatically generated label such as "Player #A3F2" — never your name, email, or contact details. Leaderboard data is used only to run the rankings and to detect cheating.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App may schedule local reminders on your device (for example, when a daily reward is ready). They are created on the device itself; no personal information is sent to us to deliver them. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.

Information Collected Through Third-Party Services

Bullet Express uses a small number of third-party services to show ads (free game), process purchases, run leaderboards, deliver updated settings, measure where installs come from, report crashes, and balance difficulty:

  • Anonymous Gameplay Analytics (Supabase): To balance difficulty and improve the game, the App may send anonymous gameplay events — such as which level and wave was played, whether it was cleared, and which upgrades were used. Events are keyed to a random identifier created on first launch; they are not linked to your identity and contain no personal information. They are stored on our backend hosted by Supabase. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • Remote Configuration (Supabase): The App may download updated configuration values (for example, balance settings and offers) from our backend. This is a download only; no personal data is required to receive it. The App's core game works offline if no connection is available.
  • AppLovin MAX (advertising): Serves interstitial and rewarded ads in the free game. AppLovin and its mediation partners (which may include Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Liftoff, Mintegral and Moloco) may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, device information, and ad-engagement data, subject to your tracking or consent choice on your platform. See AppLovin's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Adapty (in-app purchase management): Manages purchase state and entitlements (such as "Remove Ads" or an active season pass). Adapty receives an anonymous user identifier and your purchase status. We do not receive your payment details. See Adapty's Privacy Policy.
  • Singular (install attribution): Helps us understand which advertising campaign, if any, led to an install. Singular may receive your device's advertising identifier (subject to your tracking choice), device information, and install and purchase events. See Singular's Privacy Policy.
  • Sentry (crash reporting): If the App crashes or hits an error, a diagnostic report (device model, OS version, App version, and technical details of the error) may be sent so we can fix it. Reports contain no personal information. See Sentry's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple StoreKit / Google Play Billing: Processes in-app purchases, depending on where you obtained the App. See Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising & Your Tracking Choice

Bullet Express is free and shows interstitial and rewarded ads served via AppLovin MAX. If you purchase "Remove Ads" (or a bundle that includes it), interstitial ads are turned off (optional rewarded ads that grant bonuses remain available).

Ad partners may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) to show more relevant ads. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. The game plays exactly the same either way.

  • On iOS: permission is requested on first launch under Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. Change it any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android: in the European Economic Area and the UK a consent prompt is shown on first launch. You can reset or delete the identifier at any time in Settings > Google > Ads.

In-App Purchases

Bullet Express offers optional one-time purchases:

  • Gold Packs: Consumable purchases that add in-game gold.
  • Bundles and Offers: One-time purchases that grant gold, weapons, power-up parts, or other in-game items. Bundles that include "Remove Ads" are permanent for that part; the rest is consumable.
  • Remove Ads: Removes interstitial advertising permanently.
  • Monthly Card: A one-time purchase that grants gold immediately and a daily gold reward for 30 days. It does not renew automatically.
  • Season Pass: A one-time purchase that unlocks the premium reward track of the current season (and, while the season lasts, an optional faster game speed you can turn on or off in Settings). It does not renew automatically.
  • Golden Wheel: A paid spin that grants one random in-game reward. The odds for each reward are shown in the App before you buy. It may not be available in some countries.
  • Power-ups and Energy: Consumable purchases that add power-up uses or energy for the optional unlimited mode.

There are no subscriptions. Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, and permanent purchases can be restored on the same Apple ID or Google account.

How We Use Information

  • Save and resume your progress and settings
  • Show ads (free game) and process optional purchases
  • Run leaderboards and the weekly league
  • Understand anonymous, aggregate difficulty and engagement patterns to balance and improve the game
  • Measure install attribution and diagnose crashes
  • Deliver updated configuration

Data Retention

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you uninstall the App. A small progress copy kept in the device's secure storage for reinstalls on the same device can remain after uninstalling; it is never sent to us. Device backups in your own Apple or Google account are under your control. Anonymous analytics events are retained on our backend only as long as needed to balance and improve the game; leaderboard entries are kept while the ranking period and its results are shown; crash reports are retained for up to 90 days. You can request deletion as described on our Data Deletion page.

Children's Privacy

Bullet Express is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

Your Privacy Rights by Region

GDPR (European Economic Area & UK)

If you reside in the EEA or UK, you have the right to access, rectify, or delete your data, and to withdraw consent for ad personalization at any time via your device's tracking settings (ATT on iOS, Google Ads settings on Android) or the consent prompt.

CCPA (California)

We do not sell your personal information. If you reside in California, you may exercise your CCPA rights by contacting us.

LGPD (Brazil)

If you reside in Brazil, you have rights of access, correction, deletion, and consent withdrawal under the LGPD. Contact us to exercise them.
