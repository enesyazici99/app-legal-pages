Frequently Asked Questions

How does my progress work?

Your progress, gold, train, items, and settings are stored locally on your device. If you reinstall the App on the same device, your progress is restored automatically. Progress is not synced between different devices.

What happens when my train is destroyed?

Your train, gold, and upgrades are always kept. You go back to an earlier wave and can rearrange your wagons at the station, buy upgrades, and try again.

I bought "Remove Ads" but still see ads.

"Remove Ads" turns off interstitial ads (the full-screen ads between levels). Optional rewarded ads — the ones you tap to earn gold or a bonus — remain available by choice. If interstitials still appear, open Settings and tap "Restore Purchases," and make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID or Google account used for the purchase.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the in-game Settings and tap "Restore Purchases." Permanent purchases like "Remove Ads" are tied to your Apple ID or Google account and restore automatically. Consumable items (gold, power-ups, energy, wheel spins) are used immediately and cannot be restored.

I bought gold but didn't receive it.

Gold is usually granted within a few seconds. If it doesn't appear, restart the App while connected to the internet. If it's still missing, email us with your approximate purchase time and we'll help.

How do the Monthly Card and the Season Pass work?

Both are one-time purchases — there are no subscriptions and nothing renews automatically. The Monthly Card gives gold right away and a daily gold reward for 30 days; open the App each day to collect it. The Season Pass unlocks the premium rewards of the current season.

What are the Golden Wheel odds?

The odds of every reward are shown on the wheel screen before you spin.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. The core game is playable without an internet connection. A connection is used for ads, purchases, leaderboards, configuration updates, and anonymous analytics.

How do I turn off sound, music, or vibration?

Open the Settings panel (gear icon) and toggle sound, music, or vibration on or off.
