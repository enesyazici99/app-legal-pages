Data Deletion Request

Pandoku
Last Updated: August 3, 2026

This page explains how to have data associated with Pandoku deleted, what is removed, and what we are required to keep.

Pandoku has no accounts, no logins and no in-app purchases. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location, and your puzzle progress never leaves your device. There is very little for us to hold in the first place.

Option 1 — Delete data stored on your device

Your completed levels, hearts, hints and settings are stored locally on your device and are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it immediately. No request to us is needed.

Option 2 — Request deletion of advertising data

The only data that leaves your device is advertising data — your device's advertising identifier and which ads were shown or tapped — collected by AppLovin MAX and the ad networks it mediates. To request deletion:

  1. Send an email to yansingeceler2025@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request — Pandoku
  3. State the platform you played on (Google Play or App Store) and the approximate date you last played.

We will confirm receipt and complete the deletion within 30 days, and pass the request on to the advertising partners that hold the data.

You can also remove the identifier yourself at any time, which takes effect immediately:

  • Android: Settings › Google › Ads › Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID)
  • iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking — turn off tracking for Pandoku

What is deleted and what is kept

Data On request Retention
Advertising identifier and ad interaction data Deleted Otherwise subject to each ad network's own retention policy
Support email correspondence Deleted Otherwise up to 12 months
Puzzle progress, hearts, settings Not held by us Stays on your device until you uninstall
Aggregated statistics Retained Combined figures that contain no identifiers and cannot be linked back to any individual or device.

Because Pandoku collects no personal information, the data described above cannot be traced back to you by name. We may therefore need the details listed in Option 2 to locate the correct records.
