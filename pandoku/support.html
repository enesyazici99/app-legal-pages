Pandoku
Last Updated: July 29, 2026

Pandoku is a logic puzzle. Every board has exactly one solution, and every solution can be reached by reasoning — you never have to guess.

How to Play

  • Place exactly one panda in each colour region.
  • Place exactly one panda in each row and each column.
  • No two pandas may touch, including diagonally.

Controls

  • Tap a cell to mark it with an X — a note that no panda can go there.
  • Tap twice quickly to place a panda.
  • Drag across cells to mark or clear a run of them at once.
  • Placed pandas and the marks left by a mistake are locked and cannot be cleared.

Hearts, Hints and Reveals

  • You start each level with three hearts. A wrong placement costs one.
  • The bulb offers a deduction you could have made yourself, and shows which cells it rests on. Nothing happens until you press Apply.
  • The panda button places one correct panda outright.
  • When a charge runs out you can watch a short ad for another.

Common Questions

My progress disappeared. Progress is stored on the device only. Deleting the App, or restoring a backup made before you played, removes it.

A puzzle looks impossible. Every shipped board is checked to have exactly one solution reachable without guessing. If one seems otherwise, please send the level number and we will look at it.

An ad will not close. Force-quit the App and reopen it; progress on the current level is kept.
