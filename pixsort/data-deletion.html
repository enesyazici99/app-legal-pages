Data Deletion Request

PixSort Plus
Last Updated: August 3, 2026

This page explains how to have data associated with PixSort Plus deleted, what is removed, and what we are required to keep.

PixSort Plus has no accounts, no servers and no advertising. All photo analysis happens on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Your photos and their contents are never uploaded anywhere.

Option 1 — Delete data stored on your device

Your scan history, credit balance and settings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it immediately. No request to us is needed.

Your photos and albums

PixSort Plus never copies or uploads your photos — it only adds existing photos to albums in your own Photos library. Those albums and photos belong to you and remain after the app is removed. To remove them, delete the albums in the Photos app.

Photos you deleted through the app go to Recently Deleted in Photos, where iOS keeps them for up to 30 days before permanent deletion.

You can revoke photo library access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › PixSort Plus.

Option 2 — Request deletion of data held on our servers

To request deletion of the records we hold, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to yansingeceler2025@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request — PixSort Plus
  3. State the approximate date you last used the app. If you contacted support before, include that email thread so we can match the records.

We will confirm receipt and complete the deletion within 30 days.
