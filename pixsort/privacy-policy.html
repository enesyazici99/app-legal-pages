Privacy Policy

PixSort Plus: AI Photo Organizer
Last Updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes what information is collected, used and shared when you use the PixSort mobile application ("App").

Your Photos Stay on Your Device

All photo analysis, categorization and duplicate detection is performed entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework and an on-device AI model. Your photos, their contents and their metadata are never uploaded to any server or shared with any third party, including the services listed below.

Information We Collect

  • Photo Library Access: The App reads your photo library to scan, categorize and organize your photos, and changes it only when you choose to (creating albums, deleting photos). All of this happens on your device. We do not upload, store or transmit your photos.
  • On-device app data: Scan results, your settings and statistics such as photos reviewed are stored locally on your device.
  • Subscription status: If you start a trial or subscribe, our subscription service, Adapty, stores your purchase history and whether your subscription is active, linked to a random identifier the App creates on your device. Payments are processed by Apple; we never receive your payment details.
  • App usage and install measurement: Our measurement partner, AppsFlyer, receives install and session events and a few in-app milestones (for example the first scan, the first swipe, the subscription screen being shown, a purchase and its price, and errors when loading purchases). Like any internet service it also sees your IP address (from which it derives your country) and device and operating-system details. Only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt, it also receives your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). We use this to learn which advertising campaign, if any, brought the App to you. No photo or photo information is ever included.

How We Use Your Information

  • Analyze, categorize and find duplicate photos on your device
  • Organize photos into albums and delete the ones you choose
  • Provide and restore your subscription
  • Measure which advertising campaigns lead to installs and subscriptions, and find problems in the purchase flow

Third-Party Services

  • Apple StoreKit: Processes purchases. Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Adapty (subscription management): Manages subscription status and purchase history against a random identifier. Adapty Privacy Policy.
  • AppsFlyer (install attribution): Measures which advertising campaign, if any, led to an install, and the trials and purchases that followed, as described above. Adapty shares the AppsFlyer identifier so subscription events can be attributed. AppsFlyer Privacy Policy.

Tracking and Advertising

PixSort does not display any advertisements. When you first use the App, iOS asks whether the App may track you. If you allow it, AppsFlyer may use your advertising identifier to measure our own ad campaigns; if you decline, it does not receive it, and the App works exactly the same. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers above, only for the purposes described, and never your photos or their contents.

Data Retention

  • On-device app data stays on your device until you uninstall the App.
  • Adapty and AppsFlyer keep the data described above according to their own retention policies (linked above).
  • Photos organized into albums remain in your photo library and are managed by iOS.
  • Deleted photos are moved to the "Recently Deleted" album in iOS, where they remain for up to 30 days before permanent deletion.

Photo Library Permission

PixSort requires access to your photo library to function. You can manage this permission at any time in your device's Settings app under Privacy & Security > Photos. The App requests:

  • Read Access: To scan and analyze your photos for categorization and duplicate detection.
  • Write Access: To create albums and organize photos, and to delete photos when you choose to do so.

Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The App does not require any personal information to function and does not display advertising.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us so we can promptly address it.

Data Security

Your photos and on-device data are protected by your device's built-in security, including encryption and your passcode. Data sent to Adapty and AppsFlyer is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Your Privacy Rights by Region

GDPR (European Economic Area & UK)

If you reside in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the following rights under the GDPR:

  • Right to access your data
  • Right to rectification of your data
  • Right to erasure of your data
  • Right to object to data processing
  • Right to data portability

On-device data is under your control and is removed when you uninstall the App. To exercise your rights for data held by Adapty or AppsFlyer, contact us at the address below and we will handle the request with them; see also our data deletion page.

LGPD (Brazil)

If you reside in Brazil, you have the following rights under the LGPD:

  • Right to know whether your personal data is being processed
  • Right to access your data
  • Right to correct incomplete or outdated data
  • Right to delete anonymized or unnecessary data
  • Right to withdraw your consent

CCPA (California)

If you reside in California: we do not sell your personal information. If you allow tracking, your advertising identifier is shared with AppsFlyer to measure our ad campaigns; you can decline or withdraw this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
