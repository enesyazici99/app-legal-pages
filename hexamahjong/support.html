Frequently Asked Questions

How does my progress work?

Your progress, coins, lives, boosters, and settings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App removes this data. Progress is not currently synced between devices.

How do lives work?

You lose a life when you fail a level (running out of space and choosing not to continue). Lives refill over time automatically; you can also get one by watching an ad or refill them all with coins. When you run out, wait for the timer or refill to keep playing.

What are boosters?

Boosters are optional power-ups on the bar below the tray: the Hammer removes a stack from the board, the Hand swaps one stack in the tray, and Refresh replaces the whole tray. They unlock as you progress and are only spent when they actually do something — cancelling a booster costs nothing.

What happens when the board is full?

When there is no free cell left, an "Out of Space" window appears. You can clear a few stacks and keep playing by watching an ad or spending coins; otherwise the level is lost and a life is used.

I bought "No Ads" but still see ads.

"No Ads" turns off interstitial ads (the full-screen ads between levels). Optional rewarded ads — the ones you tap to earn a life, a continue, or an unlocked cell — remain available by choice. If interstitials still appear, open Settings and tap "Restore," and make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID or Google account used for the purchase.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the in-game Settings and tap "Restore." One-time purchases like "No Ads" are tied to your Apple ID or Google account and restore automatically. Consumable purchases (coins, bundles, offers) are used immediately and cannot be restored.

I bought coins but didn't receive them.

Coins are usually granted within a few seconds. If they don't appear, restart the App while connected to the internet. If they're still missing, email us with your approximate purchase time and we'll help.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Hexa Mahjong is fully playable without an internet connection. A connection is only used for ads, purchases, optional content updates, and anonymous analytics.

How do I turn off sound, music, or vibration?

Open the Settings panel (gear icon) and toggle sound, music, or vibration on or off.
