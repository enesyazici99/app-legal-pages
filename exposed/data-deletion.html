What There Is to Delete

Exposed holds no account and no personal information. Two things exist:

  • On your device: your progress, your settings and your chosen language. Deleting the App removes all of it immediately and permanently.
  • On our server: anonymous gameplay events, attached to a random number the App generated on your device. There is no name, email or advertising identifier attached to them, and nothing that can be traced back to you by us.

Deleting the Server-Side Events

Because the identifier is random and held only on your device, we cannot find your rows without it. To have them removed:

  1. Email yansingeceler2025@gmail.com from any address, with the subject Exposed data deletion.
  2. Include the device identifier shown at the bottom of the language screen in the App.

We delete the matching rows within 30 days and confirm by reply. If you have already uninstalled the App and no longer have the identifier, the events are already unlinked from you and will age out on their own within 24 months.

Purchases

Subscription records are held by Apple, Google and Adapty under their own retention policies. Deleting your gameplay events does not cancel a subscription — cancel that in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.
