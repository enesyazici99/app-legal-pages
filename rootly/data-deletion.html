Data Deletion Request

Rootly
Last Updated: August 3, 2026

This page explains how to have data associated with Rootly deleted, what is removed, and what we are required to keep.

Rootly is the one app of ours that has real accounts. Your email address and your learning progress are stored in our cloud database so they can sync across devices — so unlike our other apps, uninstalling is not enough here.

Option 1 — Delete data stored on your device

Uninstalling removes the local copy and any cached content, but your account and learning progress remain on our servers. To remove those, use Option 2 below.

Option 2 — Request deletion of data held on our servers

Deleting your account removes your email address and all learning progress from our servers. This cannot be undone.

To request deletion of your account and all data held on our servers:

  1. Send an email to yansingeceler2025@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request — Rootly
  3. State the email address on your account.

We will confirm receipt and complete the deletion within 30 days.
