Data Deletion Request

Sudoku Solitaire
Last Updated: August 3, 2026

This page explains how to have data associated with Sudoku Solitaire deleted, what is removed, and what we are required to keep.

Sudoku Solitaire has no accounts and no logins. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location, and your puzzle progress never leaves your device. The only data that leaves your device is advertising data.

Option 1 — Delete data stored on your device

Your puzzle completion status and game settings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it immediately. No request to us is needed.

Option 2 — Request deletion of data held on our servers

To request deletion of the records we hold, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to yansingeceler2025@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request — Sudoku Solitaire
  3. State the platform you played on (Google Play or App Store) and the approximate date you last used the app.

We will confirm receipt and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Advertising data

Ads are delivered by third-party networks using your device's advertising identifier. You can reset or remove that identifier yourself at any time, which takes effect immediately:

  • Android: Settings › Google › Ads › Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID)
  • iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking — turn off tracking for Sudoku Solitaire

To have data deleted from an advertising network directly, contact that network using the privacy contact listed in its own privacy policy.
