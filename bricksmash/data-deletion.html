Data Deletion Request

BrickSmash — published by ygappstudio
Last Updated: August 3, 2026

This page explains how to have data associated with BrickSmash (Brick Smash : Puzzle Blast) deleted, what is removed, and what we are required to keep.

Option 1 — Delete data stored on your device

Your game progress, coins, lives, boosters and settings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it immediately. No request to us is needed, and this also stops any further analytics from being sent.

On iOS, if you enabled iCloud sync, a backup copy may remain in your personal iCloud storage. Remove it under Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage. That copy is held in your own iCloud account — we cannot access or delete it for you.

Option 2 — Request deletion of data held on our servers

To request deletion of the records we hold, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to yansingeceler2025@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request — BrickSmash
  3. In the message, state the platform you played on (Google Play or App Store) and the approximate date you last played. If you contacted support before, include that email thread so we can match the records.

We will confirm receipt and complete the deletion within 30 days.

BrickSmash has no user accounts, logins or profiles. Our analytics records are keyed to a randomly generated identifier created on first launch and are not linked to your name, email or any personal identifier. Because of this we may need the details above to locate the correct records — and in some cases the data is anonymous enough that it cannot be traced back to an individual at all.

What is deleted and what is kept

Data On request Retention
Gameplay analytics (level attempts, shots used, session events) Deleted Otherwise kept only as long as needed for game balancing
Crash and diagnostic reports Deleted Otherwise up to 90 days
Support email correspondence Deleted Otherwise up to 12 months
Local progress, coins, settings Not held by us Stays on your device until you uninstall
Purchase and transaction records Retained Held by Google Play and Apple as the payment processors, and kept as required by tax and accounting law. We cannot delete these.
Aggregated statistics Retained Combined figures that contain no identifiers and cannot be linked back to any individual or device.

Advertising data

Advertising is delivered by third-party networks (AppLovin, Google AdMob, Unity Ads) using your device's advertising identifier. You can reset or remove that identifier yourself at any time:

  • Android: Settings › Google › Ads › Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID)
  • iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking — turn off tracking for BrickSmash

To have data deleted from an advertising network directly, contact that network using the privacy contact listed in its own privacy policy.
