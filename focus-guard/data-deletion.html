Data Deletion Request

FocusGuard
Last Updated: August 3, 2026

This page explains how to have data associated with FocusGuard deleted, what is removed, and what we are required to keep.

FocusGuard has no accounts and no servers. Your step data, the apps you choose to block, and your quest progress are all processed and stored on your device and are never transmitted anywhere. There is almost nothing for us to hold.

Option 1 — Delete data stored on your device

Your quest progress, streaks, focus scores, blocked-app selections and settings are stored locally on your device. Step count is read from Apple HealthKit and processed on-device only. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it immediately. No request to us is needed.

Revoking permissions

You can withdraw the permissions FocusGuard uses at any time, independently of deleting the app:

  • Health / step count: Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › FocusGuard
  • Screen Time / app blocking: Settings › Screen Time

Option 2 — Request deletion of data held on our servers

To request deletion of the records we hold, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to yansingeceler2025@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request — FocusGuard
  3. State the approximate date you last used the app. If you contacted support before, include that email thread so we can match the records.

We will confirm receipt and complete the deletion within 30 days.

FocusGuard has no user accounts, and the records above are keyed to anonymous identifiers rather than to your name or email. We may therefore need the details in Option 2 to locate the correct records — and in some cases the data is anonymous enough that it cannot be traced back to an individual at all.
